FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to speak Chinese to use Alibaba.com?
No. Alibaba.com itself supports English, and Chengquan's consultants can guide you through everything in English — from registration to daily operations. Buyer inquiries also come in English from international buyers.
Do I need to be physically in China to open an Alibaba.com store?
Your company needs to be registered in China (or have a Chinese entity) to open a seller account on Alibaba.com. If you're planning to set up a company in Dongguan, we can refer you to reliable business registration services and office space.
What does it cost to work with Chengquan?
Alibaba.com account fees are set nationally by Alibaba — they are the same regardless of which authorized partner you work with. Chengquan does not charge additional hidden fees. Contact us to get a clear breakdown of all costs before you decide.
Can Chengquan help me if I'm not in Dongguan?
Yes. As a member of the Alibaba.com Channel Development Committee, Chengquan has a network of trusted official partners across China. Contact us and tell us your location — we'll refer you to the right certified local partner at no extra cost.
